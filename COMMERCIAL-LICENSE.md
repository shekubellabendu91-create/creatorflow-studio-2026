# CreatorFlow Studio — Commercial License Template

**Status:** Template for discussion; not legal advice.

## 1. Product

This agreement concerns the CreatorFlow Studio source code as delivered at the time of purchase.

## 2. Proposed fee

One-time proposed license fee: **US$5,000**.

Payment terms, taxes, payment method and delivery date must be agreed in writing before payment.

## 3. Rights granted

Unless the signed agreement states otherwise, the buyer receives:

- Commercial usage rights for the delivered source code.
- Permission to customize the product.
- Permission to deploy the product for the buyer's business.
- Permission to use the product under the buyer's own branding (white-label use).

## 4. Rights not automatically transferred

This template does **not** automatically transfer copyright, intellectual-property ownership, trademarks, domain names, accounts, or unrelated code/assets. If ownership transfer is requested, it must be explicitly written into and signed as part of the final agreement.

## 5. Support

The proposed package includes 30 days of reasonable bug-fix support for the delivered version. New features, integrations, hosting and third-party services are excluded unless separately agreed.

## 6. Third-party services

Any AI APIs, hosting providers, payment processors, fonts, libraries or other third-party services remain subject to their own terms and fees.

## 7. Delivery

Delivery should include the agreed source-code repository/archive, setup instructions and any agreed deployment documentation after cleared payment and execution of the final agreement.

## 8. Final agreement

This file is a negotiation template only. Before accepting a $5,000 transaction, both parties should use a final written agreement appropriate to their jurisdiction and obtain professional legal advice where necessary.
